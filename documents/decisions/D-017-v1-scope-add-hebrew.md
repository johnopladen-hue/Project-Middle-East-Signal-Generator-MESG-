# D-017 — v1 scope amended: add Israeli Hebrew

**Date:** 2026-09-27
**Status:** Decided

## Decision

v1 covers **three** language communities: Levantine Arabic, Iranian Persian (both per [D-008](D-008-v1-source-scope.md)) and **Israeli Hebrew**. English-language outlets remain the comparison side.

## Context

Owner ruling, 2026-09-27. Israel is inside the CENTCOM view MESG already renders ([D-015](D-015-region-taxonomy-ucp-aors.md)), and it is central to the region's conflicts. Hebrew-language coverage often diverges from Israeli outlets' own English editions (Ynet↔Ynetnews, Israel Hayom he↔en, Arutz 7↔INN), and those divergences are exactly the signal MESG exists to surface.

## Options rejected

- **Stay at two communities.** Rejected by the Owner, since it leaves a central actor's in-language voice out.
- **Add Hebrew and Gulf Arabic together.** Not proposed; it breaks the "narrow v1" principle (TDD §2).

## Evidence

- Owner ruling, 2026-09-27. The candidate source list has 7 Hebrew RSS feeds and 6 Hebrew Telegram channels verified live, plus 4 Hebrew↔English same-outlet pairs.

**[D-008](D-008-v1-source-scope.md), in part** (its language list only; its reasoning and narrow-v1 principle stand). D-008's own file is marked "Amended by D-017" below its Status field.
